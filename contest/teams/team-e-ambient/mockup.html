Pomodoro, Long Braise

ポモドーロ · 長時間煮込み

Active 15 min
Total 2 h 30 min
Servings
4
Units

Ingredients

San Marzano tomatoes, crushed1.6 kg
Shallots, thinly sliced180 g
Garlic cloves, smashed8
Olive oil, Ligurian120 ml
Basil stems (tied)1 bunch
Sea salt, fineto taste
Black pepper, crackedto taste

Method

1
Warm the oil over low heat in a heavy-bottomed pot. Add shallots with a pinch of salt. Sweat slowly — no colour — until translucent.
10 min · low
2
Add garlic, stir, 30 seconds. Let the garlic perfume the oil but never brown.
30 sec
3
Pour in the tomatoes. Bring to the quietest simmer the pot will allow. Drop the basil stems. Cover, skewed.
4
Leave it. Two hours. Stir once at the hour mark to keep the bottom clean. The sauce darkens from bright red to deep brick. Finish with salt and cracked pepper.
2 h · barely simmering
Step 2 / 4
Step 2 · Aromatics
Add the smashed garlic cloves to the sweated shallots. Stir them through the warm oil for thirty seconds — you want the garlic perfumed, not coloured.

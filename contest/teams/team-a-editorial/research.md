# Research — Team A · Editorial-Magazine

Thesis for SEKAI: **a cookbook that happens to be interactive.** Printed-page rigour — columnar grids, rule lines, drop caps, margin annotations, old-style figures on prose and lining figures on tabular data. Motion is typesetting cadence, not choreography. Every surface reads like it was set by a compositor, not arranged by a designer.

The references below are 2026-current: award-winning editorial sites, magazine digital editions, independent press studios, and product leaders who treat type as the interface.

---

## 1. Are.na — arena.computer (2024–2026 redesigns)
Are.na’s ongoing refinements — margin figure-numbers, hairline rules, serif headlines flush-left against a sans lede — remain the strongest "working magazine" on the web. **What we steal:** the discipline of number-then-title-then-meta stack; figure references rendered as margin footnotes; a sidebar that is literally a list of issue sections.

## 2. It’s Nice That — Studio Feature Pieces (2025)
Their in-depth editorial features run full-width drop caps, rule-lined pull quotes set in a secondary face, and running-head navigation that doubles as a table-of-contents. **Steal:** the "running head as nav" pattern for SEKAI’s recipe list (tag rail becomes an editorial running head, not a pill-row).

## 3. The Gentlewoman — thegentlewoman.co.uk
Display serif set enormous, body set small and slow, tiny caption sans as the only non-serif. No imagery competes with type. **Steal:** the confidence to set a recipe title at 9–11rem without apology; set body at 1rem to make the title feel like an object.

## 4. Apartamento Magazine — apartamentomagazine.com
Print-first studio of Omar Sosa. The web edition uses manuscript-style centered titles, numeral-heavy margin data (issue, page, section), and literally paginates long articles. **Steal:** centered manuscript title block on recipe detail; pagination-style transitions between cook-mode steps (like turning to a next recipe spread, not sliding a card).

## 5. Cereal Magazine — readcereal.com
Minimalism weaponised. One column, generous leading, strict grid, numerals as ornament. **Steal:** the "one column, a wide margin, nothing else" detail-page layout — with the wide margin doing work (annotations, step numbers, timer glyphs).

## 6. MIT Press Reader — thereader.mitpress.mit.edu
Academic editorial treatment: drop caps, running figure numbers, footnote superscripts that open inline annotations. **Steal:** the inline annotation pattern for ingredient substitutions and unit conversions — a superscript `†` next to "flour" reveals a gloss line in the margin, not a modal.

## 7. Working Not Working Magazine — workingnotworking.com/magazine
Uses editorial typography but with serious information density — ranked lists, tabular data, real dates. Proves that a magazine can also be a reference. **Steal:** the "recipe as a reference object" stance — lists are numbered, meta is tabular, and macros are a ledger, not a chart.

## 8. Hoefler & Co / Monotype Specimen Microsites (Mercury, Chronicle, Sentinel, 2025)
The way foundry specimen pages treat type as an interface: size-ramped testers, feature-set toggles (onum/lnum, liga/dlig, swsh/ss01), literal tracking dials. **Steal:** treat SEKAI’s font-size preference (SM/MD/LG) as a specimen ramp, not a settings toggle. Make the typographic preference legible *on the page* where it is being set.

## 9. Readymag Case Study — "New York Review of Architecture" (2025)
Proof that a serious magazine can ship on web without losing the smell of ink. Uses rule-line navigation, side-anchored section markers, and a footer that reads like a colophon. **Steal:** colophon-as-footer on every page — set, printed, version, last-edit — instead of a branded footer with logo.

## 10. Linear — linear.app (2025 refresh)
Not a magazine, but the sharpest example in product of restrained motion + serif-adjacent rigour. Motion durations are 100–180ms, exponential ease, no springs. Text reveals are opacity + 4–6px y-offset, nothing more. **Steal:** the restraint ceiling — anything longer than 320ms in SEKAI is either a page transition or an error.

## 11. Are.na’s "Annual" (2024, printed + digital) — studios behind: Binocular, Linked by Air
The digital edition of a printed annual is instructive: it sets paragraph-first, image-as-figure-with-caption, and uses left-margin numerals as the nav. **Steal:** recipe cards as a left-margin numeral + title + one-line lede + gram/min meta — no thumbnails at all in the list view. Imagery, if any, belongs on the detail page as a figure, with a caption.

## 12. Offscreen Magazine — offscreenmag.com
Independent tech-culture quarterly. Digital edition keeps print hierarchy: deck → kicker → head → dek → byline → body → sidebar. **Steal:** full editorial hierarchy on recipe detail — kicker ("N° 041 · Weeknight · 28 min"), head (title), dek (lede), byline ("Set by JC · Sakai Kitchen · 2026"), body.

## 13. Robin Rendle — robinrendle.com & "The Cloisters" newsletter
Single-author editorial craft. Rule-line heavy, marginal glosses, hand-cut dashes, explicit respect for the medium. **Steal:** the singular-author voice — SEKAI should feel set by one chef, not authored by a CMS. A small colophon note on the login page: "A private kitchen. Set in Cormorant and Noto Serif JP. Printed on demand."

## 14. Dropbox Paper (archival) & Notion Editorial Mode (2025 beta)
The editorial mode in Notion’s 2025 beta — a reading-focused rendering with drop caps, centered title, serif body — is the exact "product becoming a document" move SEKAI wants. **Steal:** a toggle isn’t needed — SEKAI is *always* in editorial mode.

## 15. Frère-Jones / Commercial Type Specimen (2025)
Specimen pages that teach OpenType through interaction: click a glyph to see its alternates, hover a paragraph to see its feature stack. **Steal:** ingredient rows reveal their conversion (metric↔imperial) on hover/focus as an **alternate glyph swap**, not a unit-change animation. The number morphs like a stylistic set.

---

## Applied to SEKAI — what the references compose into

- **List page** is a table of contents, not a gallery. Left-margin numerals, title, one-line lede, tabular meta (time · yield · macros). No thumbnails.
- **Detail page** is a magazine spread. Centered manuscript title block, drop-cap first ingredient, two-column ingredients, single-column steps with margin numerals, colophon footer.
- **Cook mode** is a page turn. One step per spread, margin data (step N of M, timer, swipe-to-next-page).
- **Login** is a colophon + a single input line. Set small, set serious, no decoration beyond a hairline rule.
- **Motion** is text reveal (clip-path wipe for the wordmark, 4–6px y-shifts for body), pagination transitions for cook-mode, and unit-swap as OpenType feature change. Nothing bounces. Nothing springs.

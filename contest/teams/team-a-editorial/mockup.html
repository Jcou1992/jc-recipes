Classic Smash Burger

Two eighty-gram patties, smashed thin on a cast-iron screaming with beef tallow.

Set by JC · Sakai Kitchen, 24 · IV · 2026
Servings 4
Units
Ingredients 9 items
Method 7 steps
  1. Grind the chuck that morning, 80/20, once through the coarse plate. Salt only after smashing, never before.
  2. Divide into 80-gram balls, loose, no compaction. Rest cold.
  3. Cast iron to smoking over full flame. A film of beef tallow. No oil. 3 min · heat
  4. Place one ball. Press hard with a wide spatula; hold for 10 seconds. Season now. Repeat.
  5. After 90 seconds, edges are lace-crisp and dark mahogany. Flip once. Cheese on immediately. 90 sec · sear
  6. 30 more seconds. Cheese is obedient. Off the heat.
  7. Stack on a martin's potato bun, toasted in the remaining tallow. Double patty, pickles, onion, yellow mustard only. Serve now.

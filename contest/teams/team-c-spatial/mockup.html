Classic Smash Burger

15 min prep 12 min cook 4 servings Meat · Quick · Grill
Servings
4
Scale × 1.0
Ingredients
454 gGround chuck, 80/20
4Potato buns, split
2 tbspUnsalted butter, melted
8American cheese slices
1 tspFlaky sea salt
Steps
1

Heat a cast-iron griddle over high until smoking. Divide beef into 4 loose balls — do not compact.

2

Butter buns and toast cut-side down until deeply golden, 40 seconds. Set aside.

3

Place a beef ball on the hot griddle. Smash hard with a stiff spatula for exactly 10 seconds. Salt the top.

4

Cook 90 seconds until the edges lace and brown. Flip, top with two cheese slices, 20 seconds.

5

Stack on the toasted bun. Serve without delay.
